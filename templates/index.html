    Glossary
    BLE
    Bluetooth Low Energy — the radio phones, speakers, and IoT devices advertise on.
    mDNS
    A service a device broadcasts on your network (e.g. a printer announcing itself). No MAC, so we key by hostname.
    wifi_probe
    A device looking for a WiFi network — captured by the monitor-mode adapter. Catches devices not on your network.
    stable devices
    Devices seen 3+ times in the last 24h — consistent presence, not a drive-by. The honest inventory count.
    ever seen
    Every MAC ever detected, including drive-bys and rotating phones. Always ≥ stable devices.
    rogue / new device
    A stable device that appeared and isn't in your known baseline. You decide if it belongs.
    id (aliases)
    How many MACs/keys the fingerprinter linked to one physical device (e.g. a phone's rotated MACs, or a speaker's BLE + mDNS).
    behavior
    always-on (fixed infrastructure), transient (a visitor), rotation (a phone's rotated MAC), mobile (moving).
    privacy mode
    A phone rotating its MAC address every ~15 min for privacy. We can't track it across rotations reliably — by design.
    RSSI
    Signal strength in dBm. Stronger (closer to 0) = closer. Distance is a rough estimate (±50%).
